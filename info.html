His career spans advertising, music videos and fiction, with experience at renowned production companies such as Iconoclast, Division, Partizan and CANADA. In 2019, he co-founded Diplomats, developing a cross-disciplinary approach combining production, art direction and directing.

He has collaborated with artists including The Blaze, Pharrell Williams, Drake, Kanye West, Pusha T, Dizzee Rascal, Jamie xx, Diplo and Ibeyi, as well as brands such as Louis Vuitton, Hermès and Kenzo.

His work has been widely recognised and awarded, including a Grammy Award for Pharrell Williams’ Happy and Producer of the Year at the UK Music Video Awards. Today, he develops his own projects as a Director and Creative Director, with a cinematic and instinctive approach shaped by his Franco-Cuban roots.
